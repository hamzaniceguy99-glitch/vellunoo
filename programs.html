The programs, in detail

Four programs of 6 to 12 weeks, worked on your casting video and your own water.

Program 01 · Loops, not lassos

Casting that lands

Casting is the part everyone films badly and nobody diagnoses. We work from ten-second clips: where the rod stops, how the line loads, why the loop opens. Then roll casts, reach casts and enough distance for real water, practiced on a lawn for ten minutes a day.

  • 10 weeks
  • Beginners up
  • 10 min a day
  • Rod, line, a lawn

Start this program

Weeks 4–6 · Loop control

Tight loops into wind, open loops on purpose.

Weeks 7–8 · Roll and reach

Casting where there are trees behind you.

Weeks 9–10 · Accuracy

Hitting a dinner plate at thirty feet.

Weeks 1–2 · Structure

Reading current, depth and cover.

Weeks 3–4 · Approach

Where to stand, and where not to.

Weeks 5–6 · Drift

Mending and getting a natural presentation.

Weeks 7–8 · Working a pool

First cast to last, in order.

Program 02 · Where fish actually sit

Reading water

Send photographs of your river and we'll mark where to fish and in what order. Then the presentation side: mending, drag-free drifts and approaching a pool without announcing yourself from forty feet away.

  • 8 weeks
  • Any level
  • 3 hours a week
  • Photos of your water

Start this program

Program 03 · Stop buying randomly

Flies & hatches

Most anglers own four hundred flies and fish six. We cover the handful of insect groups that matter, seasonal hatches for your region, how to tell what fish are eating, and how to build a compact box that covers a season.

  • 8 weeks
  • Any level
  • 2 hours a week
  • Your current fly box

Start this program

Weeks 1–2 · The insects

Mayflies, caddis, stones and midges, without the Latin exam.

Weeks 3–4 · Matching

What is hatching, and what fish are taking.

Weeks 5–6 · Subsurface

Nymphs and where most trout feed.

Weeks 7–8 · Your box

Cut down to what earns its place.

Weeks 1–2 · Research

Maps, gauges, reports and regulations.

Weeks 3–4 · The plan

A day built around conditions, not hope.

Weeks 5–6 · Review

What happened, and what to change next time.

Program 04 · Arrive with a plan

New water & trips

We plan a real trip: reading flow gauges, finding legal access, timing the day, and building a short list of what to try first. And we'll tell you honestly when a local guide for one day will teach you more than a month of anything else.

  • 6 weeks
  • Some experience
  • 3 hours a week
  • A destination in mind
